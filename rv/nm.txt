WELCOME AND BIENVENIDOS TO 
THE "LAND OF ENCHANTMENT!" 
New Mexico is known for its breathtaking sunsets and 
mountain air. From high desert to aspen vistas our 35 
state parks have something for everyone! Whether it be 
a mountain hike or water skiing at one of our lake parks, 
there is always something new for you and your family to 
experience.

World-Class Fly-Fishing and Marina Amenities 
Enjoy world-class fly fishing on the Quality Waters of 
the San Juan River at Navajo Lake State Park. Camp 
in the park or picnic at one of the day use areas along 
the river. Navajo Lake offers some of the best boating, 
fishing, hiking, and camping in the state. Make sure to 
check out the award-winning Navajo Lake Marina. Rent 
a houseboat, play on the bumper boats, or dine in the 
floating restaurant. 
Group Shelters & Yurts! 
Group Shelters are a great way to come together under 
one roof. Many of our parks offer shelters of various sizes. 
Please visit your favorite parks webpage to learn more. 
Three yurts are available to rent at Hyde Memorial State 
Park, high in the Sangre de Cristo Mountains outside of 
Santa Fe. Each yurt is secure with a locked door, six cots 
and a propane heater - perfect for slumber parties or a 
basecamp for skiing the nearby ski area! Reserve one 
now at www.reserveamerica.com. 
Reserve a campsite today or buy a day-use 
pass on-line! 
Camping reservations can be made at up to 6 months in 
advance or on the day of your arrival. Just coming for a 
daytime picnic? Visitors are strongly encouraged to buy a 
day-use pass online, print it and head to the park. Hassle 
free! Go online at www.reserveamerica.com or call 
1-877-664-7787 to make your reservations. Parks do
accept exact cash or checks, but only at pay stations.
Remember, New Mexico is beautiful and rural- there
is often no ATM near a park.
Know Before You Go! 
New Mexico State Parks encourages everyone to visit 
www.emnrd.state.nm.us/SPD to learn more about 
seasonal closures, fire restrictions and other safety 
notifications before visiting a park. Please find your park 
online and access "Park Alerts" for the most up-to-date 
information. Safe Travels! 
Love Your State Parks? Lend a Hand by 
Volunteering! 
Volunteers are critical to the success of our parks. We are 
always needing volunteers for a variety of tasks including 
camp hosting, staffing our visitor centers, hosting park 
events, maintaining facilities and more. Share your skills 
today. Apply online at https://www.emnrd.nm.gov/spd/ 
volunteering/.
