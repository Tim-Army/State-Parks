Hampton Beach RV Park
HAMPTON
SNME
This campground has 28 full-hookup and 50 dry camping 
(no-hookup RV) sites and is located at the mouth of the 
Hampton River, at the edge of the Atlantic Ocean.
Jericho Mountain State Park
BERLIN
SLNMWIQMIJSE
The campground has 20 primitive sites including ATV  
sites, five cabins, six tent sites and nine RV sites. There  
are over 75 miles of OHRV trails at the park, a day-use  
area with a variety of recreational opportunities. 
Lafayette Place Campground
FRANCONIA
NMIJE
In the heart of Franconia Notch State Park is this 97-site 
wooded campground. It is a great base for hiking, biking  
and exploring the White Mountain region.
Lake Francis State Park
PITTSBURG
SNM PWEIQSE
In the Great North W oods, this lakefront park offers 47  
sites for camping, including ATV sites, five walk-to sites  
as well as some two-way hookups. The park offers an 
excellent base for fishing, wildlife viewing and kayaking. 
Milan Hill State Park
MILAN
L WEJ
With sweeping views of the White Mountains and of 
mountains in Maine and Canada, this park offers 10  
campsites including four yurts for primitive camping.
Mollidgewock State Park
ERROL
WISE
Situated along the banks of the Androscoggin River,  
this park has 44 tent campsites. The park is a favorite  
of canoe and kayak enthusiasts.
Monadnock State Park
JAFFREY
NMJ
The Gilson Pond Campground has 40 tent or pop-up  
sites, including four hookup sites and five remote walk-to 
sites. Y ear-round camping is available.
Bear Brook State Park
ALLENSTOWN
NMPWEIMIJSE
This 10,000-acre park has 101 wooded campsites located  
on Beaver Pond. There are miles of trails for a wide  
variety of recreational opportunities.
Cannon Mountain RV Park
FRANCONIA
SPI MIJSE
Located at the north end of Franconia Notch State  
Park, it offers 7 full-hookup campsites. The RV Park  
is open year-round with limited services.
Coleman State Park
STEWARTSTOWN
LNMPWEIJSE
On Little Diamond Pond, this 25-site campground  
includes sites for ATV . The park also has year- 
round cabin rentals that can sleep up to 12 people.  
Deer Mountain Campground
PITTSBURG
L WJSE
Just south of the Canadian border, this 25-site  
primitive campground is located in Connecticut  
Lakes State Forest. 
Dry River Campground
HARTS LOCATION
NMWJ
This campground has 38 sites with six walk-to sites  
in a woodland setting, located in the heart of  
Crawford Notch State Park.
Ellacoya State Park
GILFORD
SNM PEME
The 38 full-hookup sites on the shore of Lake 
Winnipesaukee offer mountain views. 
Greenfield State Park
GREENFIELD
NMPWEIMJSE
Tucked away in this 400-acre park are ponds, bogs  
and a 174-site campground. The campground has a 
separate beach.
Moose Brook State Park
GORHAM
NMWMIJE
With 59 campsites, the park is ideally located for fishing 
in the Peabody and Moose Rivers. The many trails in the 
park are great for exploring on foot or mountain bike. 
Mount Sunapee State Park
NEWBURY
WJ
The campground has 10 campsites including five lean-to 
sites and five platform sites. The sites are wooded and are 
for tent camping. Enjoy the state beach on Lake Sunapee.
Pawtuckaway State Park
NOTTINGHAM
LNMEIMIJSE
The campground offers 193 wooded campsites, including 
five cabins, one walk-to site and two family group sites, 
many providing views of the lake. 
Pillsbury State Park
WASHINGTON
WEIIJSE
This 41-site primitive campground set within a 4,500 acre 
preserve includes canoe-to and walk-in sites. The park  
is heavily wooded with several ponds and wetlands that 
offer a variety of wildlife.
Umbagog Lake State Park
CAMBRIDGE
SLNMPWIMSE
The campground offers 65 sites with tent and two-way 
hookup sites, remote sites and cabins. The park is great 
for wildlife viewing. 
White Lake State Park
TAMWORTH
NMPEIMJSE
This 202-site campground, with two family group sites,  
is located on the shore of White Lake with some of the 
best swimming in the White Mountain region.
